About

I'm a civil engineer with an MSc in Structural Materials, working at the point where materials science meets construction practice. My focus sits across geopolymer fire assessment, concrete admixtures, waterproofing and insulation, and construction chemicals.

Alongside that, I work on sustainable and green concrete production — finding mix designs that lower embodied carbon while still meeting the strength, durability and fire performance a structure needs over its lifetime.

In short: I care about what happens to concrete long after it's poured — how it responds to fire, moisture, and the environment it's built into.
