About Me

Civil Engineering meets
Advanced Construction Materials.

I am a Civil Engineer with more than 10 years of professional experience spanning site supervision, technical consultancy, construction chemicals, concrete technology and project execution.

My professional background combines practical construction experience with specialized knowledge of concrete admixtures, waterproofing systems, insulation, structural strengthening, repair materials and sustainable construction technologies.

My academic research focuses on low-carbon and fire-resistant geopolymer materials, with particular interest in alkali-activated materials, microstructural evolution and high-temperature behavior.

I also have experience developing technical proposals, supporting clients, conducting site investigations, supervising construction activities and managing quality control.

Research

Structural Materials &
Sustainable Construction

My research focuses on sustainable and high-performance construction materials, with particular emphasis on geopolymer systems, fire resistance, microstructural development and low-carbon construction.

Geopolymer Fire Resistance

Assessment of pressed geopolymer blocks under elevated temperatures and investigation of their thermal stability.

Alkali-Activated Materials

Research involving fly ash, silica fume, waste glass powder and different Si/Al molar ratios.

Low-Carbon Construction

Sustainable construction materials and approaches aimed at reducing environmental impact.

Microstructural Analysis

Investigation of material evolution using XRD, FTIR, SEM and related characterization techniques.

High-Temperature Behavior

Evaluation of geopolymer material transformation at 600°C, 800°C and 1000°C.

Waste-Based Materials

Investigation of industrial and recycled materials for sustainable construction applications.

MSc Thesis

Fire Resistance Assessment of Geopolymer Pressed Blocks with Different Si/Al Molar Ratios

MSc research investigating pressed geopolymer blocks manufactured using fly ash, silica fume and waste glass powder. The research evaluates the influence of Si/Al molar ratio on material performance and microstructural evolution under high-temperature exposure.
