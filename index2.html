About Me

Engineering materials.
Solving construction problems.

I am a Civil Engineer specializing in structural materials, sustainable construction, concrete technology, and construction chemicals.

My professional and research interests combine practical construction engineering with advanced materials research, particularly geopolymer systems, fire-resistant materials, concrete admixtures, waterproofing, insulation, and sustainable concrete.

My approach focuses on understanding material behavior, improving performance, reducing environmental impact, and developing practical engineering solutions.

Research

Research Interests

My research focuses on sustainable structural materials, advanced cementitious systems, geopolymerization, fire performance and environmentally responsible construction technologies.

Geopolymer Fire Assessment

High-temperature behavior, thermal stability, fire resistance and microstructural transformation of geopolymer materials.

Sustainable Concrete

Development of green concrete using industrial by-products, recycled materials and reduced cement content.

Waste-Derived Materials

Utilization of fly ash, silica fume, waste glass powder and recycled aggregates in construction.

Microstructural Analysis

XRD, FTIR, SEM and EDS analysis for understanding material structure and reaction mechanisms.

Thermal Performance

Thermal degradation, heat exposure and performance of construction materials at elevated temperatures.

Life Cycle Assessment

Environmental assessment of construction materials, embodied carbon and sustainable production.

Featured Research

Geopolymer Fire Resistance

A research project investigating the fire resistance and thermal behavior of pressed geopolymer blocks with different Si/Al molar ratios.

Geopolymer • Fire • Structural Materials

Fire Resistance Assessment of Geopolymer Pressed Blocks

Investigation of geopolymer blocks manufactured using fly ash, silica fume, waste glass powder and alkaline activation. The study evaluates mechanical properties, microstructure and behavior after high-temperature exposure.
